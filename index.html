Pedro writes letters he never sends, paints abstract pieces, and pretends he's a musician. Photography and video are his favourite ways to tell stories — for now.

Born in Porto, based between London and Porto. Multidisciplinary artist and creative professional with international experience across photography, curation, and event production.

His practice is rooted in research and driven by curiosity — experimental, emotional, deeply personal. He believes every project deserves the right medium, so he keeps learning new ones.
