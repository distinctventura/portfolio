Pedro writes letters he never sends, paints abstract pieces, and pretends he's a musician. Photography and video are his favourite ways to tell stories.

Born in Porto, based between London and Porto. Multidisciplinary artist and creative professional with international experience across photography, curation, event production, and art direction.

His work is experimental, emotional, and deeply personal — always evolving. His practice isn't about having it all figured out; it's about the journey, the questions, and the messy, beautiful process of creating.
